Categories leases often exclude (check your own lease)

Only your lease decides what is recoverable. These categories show up in exclusion lists often enough to be worth checking against yours:

  • Capital improvements or replacements (some leases allow them only if amortized over their useful life)
  • Leasing commissions, advertising for vacant space, and tenant improvements for other tenants
  • The landlord's mortgage interest, principal, ground rent and depreciation
  • Costs repaid by insurance, warranties or other tenants
  • Repairing original construction defects
  • Legal fees from disputes with other tenants or lenders
  • Fines and penalties caused by the landlord's violations
  • Salaries of personnel above the building manager level
  • Reserves for future spending, where the lease doesn't permit them
